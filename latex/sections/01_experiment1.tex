\subsection{Method}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
    \item Read \texttt{ESP.getCycleCount()} before and after a loop of unrolled operations.
    \item Measure an empty loop first (baseline overhead) and subtract it from the payload measurement.
    \item Use \texttt{volatile} variables to stop the compiler from optimizing the loop away.
    \item Repeat 10 times and record min/median cycles; also compare $N=10$ vs $N=1000$ repetitions.
\end{itemize}
